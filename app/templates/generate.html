Print & Assembly Specifications

How to attach generated QR codes to student physical voting slips

  • 1
    Print QR PNGs: Extract the downloaded ZIP archive. Print images using standard letter/A4 paper or sticker sheets. Each image includes a cut-guide border.
  • 2
    Attach to Physical Slip: Glue or tape one unique QR code to the back of each physical student slip.
  • 3
    Student Writes Choice: Students manually write their desired monitor candidate on the front of the slip.
  • 4
    Authenticate at Ballot Box: The proctor scans the back QR code using the website scanner to confirm the slip is genuine before accepting it.

Copy Resistance & One-Time Redemption

A printed QR code on paper can theoretically be photocopied. To prevent paper reuse or ballot stuffing, the system supports One-Time Redemption: once a physical slip is submitted, clicking "Mark as Redeemed" flags that Slip ID in the database so any subsequent duplicate photocopy is instantly flagged as ALREADY REDEEMED!
